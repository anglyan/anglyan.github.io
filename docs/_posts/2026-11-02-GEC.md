This past Monday I attended the joint meeting from the APS's Division of Plasma Physics and the Gaseous Electronics Conference, which took place in Chicago. Neither of them is my go-to conference: whenever we use plasmas it is in the context of materials synthesis, and in particular atomic layer deposition, so we tend to gravitate towards materials science conferences instead.

However, the GEC was one of the first international conferences I attended, and probably the first one I went solo: in September 2004, as a baby scientist, I flew to Bunratty Ireland and presented a poster on our simulations of the electron energy distribution functions in Argon plasmas. Everybody from the European plasma community was there, and I remember witnesing some lively discusions on arcane subjects such as the existence of a presheath (don't worry, it's fine). 

Twenty two years later, I am back as an invited speaker at GEC giving a presentation on AI for plasma processing. What has changed? What has *not* changed?
